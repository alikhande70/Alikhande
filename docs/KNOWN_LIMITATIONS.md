# Known Limitations

- This environment does not contain MetaEditor/MetaTrader, so the current candidate has **not** passed a real MQL5 compile gate.
- Economic Calendar history for Strategy Tester is not bundled yet.
- Historical win probability remains disabled until a sufficient independent outcome sample exists.
- Shadow outcome tracking currently resolves TP/SL/expiry from live ticks; MFE/MAE sampling is not complete.
- No claim of profitability or future trading success is made.
